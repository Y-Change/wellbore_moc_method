Conclusion In this paper, the effect of the wellbore parameters on the wellhead water hammer were studied for the first time based on the wellbore transient flow model establishment. In addition, water hammer signals from field cases are also studied, and the waveform characteristics related with wellbore parameters influence was analyzed, providing some useful guidelines for the water hammer diagnostics. First, due to the line pack effect of the wellbore, a slow change trend of the water hammer waveform is showed rather than a horizontal one between the time of pump shut-in and the downhole boundary reflection. The wellbore cementing accelerates the change of the pressure trend, and the moment it occurs in the waveform depends on the length of the wellbore cementing. The wellbore diameter reduction will appear as a pressure spike on the water hammer waveform detail, while the diameter expansion will appear as a pressure sinking.
