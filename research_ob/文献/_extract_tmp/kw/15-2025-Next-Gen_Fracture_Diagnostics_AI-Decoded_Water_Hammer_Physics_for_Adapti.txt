p.7: 7 ===== SPE-230381-MS 7 allows for the estimation of fracture properties by analyzing the proximity and vector relationships between data points in this compressed representation. 3. The method is particularly powerful for conducting comparative analysis. It can reliably track relative changes in fracture parameters (e.g., an increase in width or length) between different stages (e.g., mini frac vs. main frac) on the same well, providing valuable insights into the fracture's evolution. 4. The technique offers a significant practical advantage by generating a well-informed initial approximation of fracture parameters. This can drastically improve the convergence speed and accuracy of subsequent, more precise, inverse problem-solving methods. 5. The conclusion rightly acknowledges the model's simplifications (e.g., representing the fracture as a lumped impedance) and the significant uncertainties inherent in fracture diagnostics. The method is positioned as a supplementary tool for evaluation rather than a definitive standalone solution, highlighting a scientifically honest approach.
