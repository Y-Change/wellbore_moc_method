p.14: 14 ===== November 2025 SPE Journal6926 Uncertainty Analysis. The discrepancy between fracture surface areas estimated by WHA and UTA can also arise from multiple sources of uncertainty, including model assumptions, measurement quality, reservoir heterogeneity, and fundamental differences in the physical behavior that each method captures. Understanding these limitations is critical to interpreting diagnostic results and guiding stimulation optimization. Model Assumptions. WHA typically assumes planar, symmetrical, biwing fractures with uniform height, which may oversimplify the true geometry of multicluster fractures in horizontal wells. These assumptions can lead to overestimations of fracture dimensions, espe- cially in zones affected by asymmetric growth or fracture interaction. UTA, by contrast, does not explicitly define fracture geometry but infers the effective contributing area from flow behavior, often assuming linear or bilinear flow regimes. When nonlinear flow, delayed connectivity, or progressive fracture closure is present, UTA may underestimate the area actively participating in production. Measurement Errors.
