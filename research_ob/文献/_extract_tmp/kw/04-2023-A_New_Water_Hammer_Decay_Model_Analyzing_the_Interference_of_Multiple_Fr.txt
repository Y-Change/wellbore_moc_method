The control system is a screw pump, through which the liquid is pumped into the piping system, and the instantaneous stopping of the screw pump simulates the water hammer effect at the end of hydraulic fracturing. The collection system is a pressure monitor connected to the outlet of the screw pump at 2 m and the collection software. The range of the pressure monitor is 0 to 5 MPa, with an accuracy of 0.01% of full scale and a frequency response of 10 kHz. The fracture simulation system consists of three valve- connected stubs.
